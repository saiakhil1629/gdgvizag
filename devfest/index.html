Build, learn and meet in Vizag

DevFest is Google Developer Groups' flagship community event, run by local volunteers worldwide. GDG Vizag has hosted it every year since 2018, usually at GITAM in Rushikonda.

Expect practical sessions on AI, Gemini, Google Cloud, Flutter, Android and the web, from engineers who ship for a living. Afternoons are for code labs, so you leave with something running on your laptop.
