Good to know

When and where is DevFest Vizag 2026?

Not announced yet. Past editions took place at GITAM University, Rushikonda, Visakhapatnam, in November or December. Follow GDG Vizag for the announcement.

Who can attend?

Students, developers, professionals, freelancers and entrepreneurs. All skill levels are welcome.

Is DevFest free?

Each chapter sets its own pricing. Vizag has run free and paid editions. Pricing for 2026 will be on the ticket page when registration opens.

When should I register?

As soon as tickets open. Past editions drew hundreds of RSVPs, and seats fill up.

How do I get tickets?

Tickets are released on the GDG Vizag event page on gdg.community.dev. Check the listing when it opens.

Can I speak or volunteer?

Yes. Calls for speakers and volunteers are shared on GDG Vizag channels before each edition.

What should I bring?

A laptop and charger for the code labs, and your ticket or QR code for check-in.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. DevFest follows the Google Developer Groups community guidelines. Be kind, be inclusive, keep it professional.
