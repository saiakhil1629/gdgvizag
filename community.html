Local2Global Hack : GDGVIZAG

5–6 November 2026 · 9:00 AM IST · Gayatri Vidya Parishad College of Engineering (Autonomous), Visakhapatnam

A 24-hour hackathon for Gayatri Vidya Parishad students in Visakhapatnam. Spot a local problem, build a solution with Google tools, and compete for prizes. Expect tech sessions, mentorship and fun activities, with a focus on projects that matter globally.

Everything happens on this website. Register, build your team, then go through four stages: lock your team, write your problem statement and idea, add a Gemini-made image and a Google Slides link for the jury, then add your GitHub and live website links. Both links must work. Only Gayatri Vidya Parishad students can take part.

Hackathon themes (problem statements)

Pick one of the 8 tracks. Every pitch must answer: Local, which Vizag problem and who does it affect? Global, how can it work in any city or country?
